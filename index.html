How it works

  1. Pull into the bay, or book a time ahead if you'd rather not wait.
  2. The test takes about 15 minutes.
  3. If your car passes, we send the certificate to the DMV electronically. You can renew your registration right away.
  4. If it doesn't pass, we explain why. Come back within 30 days and your retest is free.
